### [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) 
For personal knowledge management and more

### [realtimecolors.com](https://www.realtimecolors.com) 
For choosing and testing color and font combinations

### [OBS](https://obsproject.com/) 
For screen recording and streaming

### [SyncThing](https://syncthing.net/)
For easily syncing folders across devices. Can also be used to sync Obsidian on your smartphone.

### [Aegis Authenticator](https://getaegis.app/) (Android only)
Free and open source authenticator app. Ad-free, no cloud, you own your keys (back up however you wish), not tied to a phone number or anything

### [VLC Media Player](https://www.videolan.org/):
It just works. It can play virtually any video or audio format you throw at it without needing extra codecs (including videos from your own media server)

### [Web Video Caster](https://www.webvideocaster.com/home)
Any video you can play in your phone's browser, you can now play on your smart TV.
