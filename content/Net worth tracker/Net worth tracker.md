## How it works

The goal is to figure out what to do once, set it up nicely, and move on with your life. After some years, you will be rich if you consistently save money. 

The biggest lever you can pull in the beginning is your **savings rate** - % of your NET income that you can save and invest every month.

 >[!info]
 >
>**If you don't earn enough**, the sheet will tell you, and you should either reduce spending or increase your income. That's your new focus.
>
>**Rule of thumb:** if your bare essentials (housing/food/transport) cost more than 80% of your NET income, you are not going to make meaningful progress. You need some fun money and a decent savings rate.
>
>**Bonus rule of thumb:** 
>If you consistently save and invest **20% of your income**, you can retire in **30 years** (starting from 0).
>If you **invest 10% instead**, you will retire in **41 years** 
>
>Now take a guess athow long it will take if you **save 50%**? - It's still going to take **15 years**. Here is your mathematical proof that you cannot get rich quickly! ( ˇ෴ˇ )

### Step 0 - Get your bearings

- Fill a row in the Tracker every time you earn your income.
- Know monthly essential spending (only bare essentials). Track all your spending for 1-2 months to learn if needed.
- Allocate monthly fun money (start small but have it, we are not robots)
- Think about how you want to live and know your monthly rich life spending
- If income is not enough for essential expenses +30%, you need to earn more or reduce spending on things you don't care about (usually earn more)

**Pro Tip:** Occasionally (when things change - like having kids or moving), track all spending for 1-2 months to stay on top of your expenses

### Step 1 - Safety: Build an emergency fund

- Use the emergency fund category in the tracker
- Save 3 months of essential expenses in an accessible place
- Later progress to 6 months of essential expenses
- Emergency fund lives in an accessible place earning small interest (savings account, money market fund)
- Only touch that money if there is an emergency! This will allow you to act without crippling worry.

### Step 2 - Freedom: Pay off bad debt

- Bad debt is high interest debt (usually everything excluding your mortgage)
- EVERYTHING you save beyond the minimal emergency fund goes to paying off debt (it will get easier each month)

### Step 3 - Wealth: Invest your money

- More appreciating assets, fewer depreciating assets!
- Invest in your/your family's education
- Use retirement accounts to avoid taxes
- Buy total market index funds (VTI, VXUS), target date funds or similar
- Only use 0-5% for high risk investment if you really have to

### Step 4 - Automation: Set and forget

- Once you find your rhythm, starting from step 3, automate it! 
- Get paid -> Automatically perform steps 1-3 -> you live using the rest of the money

### Key principle - Pay yourself first! 

- As soon as you get income, do steps 1-3 first, then live your life using the rest of the money
- Better yet, automate that process once you find a good rhythm and live your life stress free, while building wealth in the background
